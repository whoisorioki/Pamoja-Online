# Nguvu Pamoja Online — Participant Guide

*A plain-language guide to using the online companion to the Friday sessions.*

## 1. What is this?

Nguvu Pamoja Online is the online companion to the Nguvu Pamoja Friday
session — a Fountwood Community Support Program. If you cannot attend in
person, this site lets you still take part that week:

- see the week's **theme and scripture**,
- **join the video session**,
- do your **weekly check-in** (one challenge, one victory),
- keep **private journal notes**, and
- take part in the **gender-separated forum**.

Everything works **anonymously** — no account, no email, no real name needed.

## 2. Your anonymous token (your "login")

The site has no accounts. Instead, it gives you a **3-word token** — a random
phrase like `hope-peace-grace`.

- **Generate it once** from the Home page ("Generate 3-Word Token").
- **Write it down and keep it safe.** It cannot be recovered if lost —
  nobody can look it up for you.
- Your token is stored **only in your own browser**. No real identity is ever
  connected to it, and nothing in the system maps a token to a person.
- If you clear your browser data or switch phones, **paste the same token
  back in** on the Home page to return to your check-ins and journal.

> ⚠️ **Treat your token like a password.** Anyone who has it can see and edit
> your private check-ins and journal entries.

## 3. Week pages & video session

Each week page (`/week/1` … `/week/8`) shows the theme, scripture, reflection
questions, and the **Friday video room**.

- Click **"Join Video Session"** to open the meeting inside the page.
- A direct **"Open in New Tab"** link is also shown — useful on slow
  connections or if the embedded window does not load.
- **The room is private.** The facilitator shares the room password
  separately before the session — never share it outside the group.
- If the video fails to start, the page shows a plain-language message with
  the new-tab link; you can also contact your facilitator.

## 4. Weekly check-in

On `/check-in/1` … `/check-in/8` you can record, for that week:

- your **biggest challenge** or struggle, and
- one **victory** or moment of growth.

Your check-ins are private — only readable and editable by someone holding
your token. After saving, you'll see a confirmation (nothing saves silently).

## 5. Private journal

`/journal` is your private reflection space. You can:

- write a note for any of the 8 weeks,
- view your **past journal entries and check-ins**, and
- **edit or delete** your own entries.

Journal entries are protected by your token. Nobody else can read them —
and they are automatically deleted after **90 days** by the platform.

## 6. Forum (gender-separated)

The forum has two private spaces: **Men's Space** and **Women's Space**.

- On your first visit, **choose your space** once. That choice is remembered
  only on your device. If you need to change it, ask a facilitator.
- You'll be asked for the **space passphrase** that the facilitator shares
  (rotated each 8-week cycle — again, don't share it outside the group).
- You post under a **nickname you choose** — never your token, and no
  real names needed. Your last nickname is remembered and prefilled, and you
  can change it on any post.
- You can **reply** to other posts and **flag/report** a post that worries
  you — a facilitator reviews flags privately.
- Remember the group guidelines: **grace, not judgment** — support, don't
  compare.

## 7. Resources & support contacts

The **Resources** page (`/resources`) is always visible and never gated:
crisis and support contacts including the National Addiction Helpline,
NACADA, and emergency numbers, plus the Fountwood counseling team. Please
use it if you or someone you know needs help now.

## 8. Privacy — what the platform does and doesn't collect

| The platform ... | Detail |
|---|---|
| Has **no accounts** | No sign-up, no email, no password — just your 3-word token |
| **Never asks your real name** | Forum uses a nickname you choose; the token is your credential |
| **Does not log IP addresses** | No IP logs are recorded anywhere in the system |
| **Separates spaces by gender** | Enforced technically, not just by page design |
| **Auto-deletes after 90 days** | Check-ins and journal entries are removed automatically |
| **Keeps moderation private** | Flags are reviewed by a facilitator; no public call-outs |

Nothing in the database maps a token to a real person. Even the operator
cannot look up "who you are" — the data was designed so that question
literally has no answer.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**I lost my token. Can you help me get it back?**
No — and that's a feature. Recovery would require storing your identity,
which the program deliberately does not do. Keep it in a place you won't
lose (e.g., a notes app or written copy).

**I'm using a new phone. How do I get my data back?**
Return to the Home page and **paste your saved token**. Your check-ins and
journal will be available again. Forum space choice and nickname are stored
per-device, so you may need to choose your space again and re-enter the
passphrase (a facilitator can help).

**The video meeting won't start / shows a message.**
Click **"Open in New Tab"** — that bypasses the embedded window and works on
more devices and networks. Still stuck? Contact your facilitator; they can
share a direct room link.

**What is the 90-day deletion?**
To keep long-term risk low, the platform automatically deletes check-ins and
journal entries older than 90 days. This is intentional, not a bug.

**Can participants talk to each other across the men's/women's spaces?**
No. The two spaces are kept separate at the database level, same as at the
Friday sessions.

## 10. Group values, online

The same guidelines that apply in the room apply here:

- **Confidentiality** — what is shared stays in the group; don't repeat
  others' posts offline.
- **Anonymity** — first names or pseudonyms only; never share your token.
- **Grace, not judgment** — support, don't compare.
- **No unsolicited advice** — reply with encouragement, leave correction to
  the facilitators.

*If something on the platform ever feels wrong or unsafe, tell a facilitator
or use the emergency contacts on the Resources page.*
